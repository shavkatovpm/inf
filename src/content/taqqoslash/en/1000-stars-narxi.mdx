## Is there a discount in the 1,000-Star package?

Both compared services publish a flat per-unit rate. Buying 1,000 Stars therefore does not lower the price of one Star relative to a smaller package. The total is larger because the quantity is larger, not because a special volume discount applies.

Base the decision on two questions instead of asking whether the bigger package is cheaper:

1. Do you actually need 1,000 Stars?
2. Which service currently publishes the lower final total?

If you need a different quantity, calculate it with the [Stars calculator](/en/kalkulyator).

## What matters besides price?

The lowest total is not the only criterion. StarsJoy publishes support for UzCard, HUMO, Click and Payme; Uzgets publishes UzCard, HUMO and Click. Delivery claims, guarantees and publicly visible reviews differ as well. The [Stars service ranking](/en/reyting/telegram-stars) applies a predefined formula to all of them.

If price comes first, use the lowest total in the table. If payment coverage or public trust evidence matters more, do not decide on price alone.

## What should I check before buying?

Before confirming payment in the bot, verify that:

- the selected quantity is exactly **1,000 Stars**;
- the recipient username has no typo;
- the final total matches the current table value;
- the bot username is listed on the service’s official website.

Delivering Stars does not require a password, SMS code or 2FA password. If this is your first purchase, the [Stars buying guide](/en/qollanma/stars-qanday-sotib-olinadi) explains the steps.
