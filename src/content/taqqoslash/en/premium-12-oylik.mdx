## Why the service choice matters most here

On short terms the gap between services runs to a few thousand soums — barely noticeable. As the term grows, so does the spread: on the annual plan the distance between the cheapest and dearest offer is several times wider than at other terms.

Put differently, comparing is worth little in money when you buy three months and worth the most when you buy a year. Checking the figures in the table before paying genuinely pays off here.

## Who the annual plan suits

This term is for people who use Premium **continuously**. If you are not certain, [six months](/en/taqqoslash/premium-6-oylik) may work out better: the per-month difference is not large, and you tie up half as much money.

Buying it as a gift is another reason not to rush into the annual plan — when the recipient's habits are unknown, a [shorter term](/en/taqqoslash/premium-3-oylik) is safer.
