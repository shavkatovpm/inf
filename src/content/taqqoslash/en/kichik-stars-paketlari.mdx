Many Telegram Stars purchases don't start with a large amount — they start with exactly 50 or 100, which is often enough to unlock paid content, send a small gift or pay inside a mini app. These two quantities are worth comparing on their own, since services publish them as separate packages.

## What are these amounts typically used for?

The most common uses for Stars are unlocking paid content in a channel or bot, sending a small gift to another user, and paying inside a mini app. Most of these actions are one-off and require a small amount, so 50 or 100 Stars is often enough — a larger package only makes sense for regular use or ongoing support of a creator.

## Can I buy fewer than 50 Stars?

Both compared services publish 50 as their smallest package. A smaller quantity is not listed on their public price pages — that means unpublished, not unavailable. If you need exactly that, it's worth asking directly in the bot.

## Is 100 Stars cheaper per unit than 50?

No. Both services publish the same per-Star rate regardless of package size, as shown in more detail in the [Stars package comparison](/en/taqqoslash/stars-paketlari). The total for 100 is larger than for 50 simply because the quantity is double, not because of a volume discount. If you need more, check the [1,000 Stars price](/en/taqqoslash/1000-stars-narxi) separately.

## What matters besides price?

The lowest total is not the only criterion. StarsJoy publishes support for UzCard, HUMO, Click and Payme; Uzgets publishes UzCard, HUMO and Click. Delivery claims, guarantees and publicly visible reviews differ as well — the [Stars service ranking](/en/reyting/telegram-stars) applies a predefined formula to all of them.

## What should I check before buying?

Before confirming payment in the bot, verify that:

- the selected quantity is exactly 50 or 100;
- the recipient username has no typo;
- the final total matches the current table value;
- the bot username is listed on the service's official website.

Delivering Stars does not require a password, SMS code or 2FA password. If this is your first purchase, the [Stars buying guide](/en/qollanma/stars-qanday-sotib-olinadi) explains the steps.
