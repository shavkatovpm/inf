## Why a longer term costs less per month

A service processes a long plan once: payment and activation are a single operation. That cost is then spread across all the months in the package. The longer the term, the less falls on any individual month.

The decline is **not uniform**, though. Moving from a short term to a mid-length one usually produces the largest difference, while the step after that saves considerably less. The note under the table calculates which step is actually worth the most.

## Which term to choose

The choice depends less on money than on **how long you will use it**:

- **Trying Premium out** — take the shortest plan available. The monthly cost is highest, but you are not paying for months you will not use.
- **Using it continuously** — the longest plan is cheapest per month. You pay more at once and less for each month.
- **Not sure** — look at the mid-length term. In most cases that step delivers the biggest saving without demanding a long commitment.

If you are buying it as a gift, let the recipient's habits decide: you can start with a short plan and extend later.

## What to check before buying

Once the term is settled, the remaining differences sit in the **conditions** rather than the price. Before paying, check the service's own page for payment methods, the stated delivery time and what happens if something goes wrong.

That information is collected for every compared service on the [prices page](/en/narxlar) and in the ranking built on published [criteria](/en/mezonlar).

Whatever term you pick, one safety rule holds: **no service needs your Telegram password, SMS code or 2FA password.** Activation runs on the username alone.
