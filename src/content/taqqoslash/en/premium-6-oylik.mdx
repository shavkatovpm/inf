## Why the biggest saving sits at six months

The monthly cost falls as the term grows, but the decline is **not uniform**.

Moving from three months to six drops the monthly figure noticeably — the steepest jump anywhere in the range. After that, from six to twelve, the decline slows markedly: you pay twice as much while the saving on each month stays comparatively small.

That makes six months a sensible point for most buyers: the bulk of the discount is already captured and the commitment is still short.

## Who six months does not suit

- **You are still trying Premium out** — [three months](/en/taqqoslash/premium-3-oylik) asks for less money.
- **You use Premium constantly** — [twelve months](/en/taqqoslash/premium-12-oylik) is cheaper per month, and the choice of service makes a larger difference in money there.
