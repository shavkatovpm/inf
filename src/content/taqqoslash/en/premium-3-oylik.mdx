## Who the three-month term suits

The term makes sense in two situations:

- **You are trying Premium out.** Longer than a single month, without committing for a year.
- **You are buying a gift.** If you do not know whether the recipient will use Premium, a shorter term is safer — it can be extended later.

If you are confident you will use Premium continuously, three months turns out to be the most expensive route: you pay more for every month you get.

## How much the service choice matters here

At this term the three services sit close together — the gap runs to a few thousand soums. That means the decision does not have to rest on price alone: payment methods, the stated delivery time and refund terms all count.

Those criteria are compared in the [service ranking](/en/reyting/telegram-premium), and the calculation is set out openly on the [criteria](/en/mezonlar) page.

## Compared with the other terms

Moving from three months to six drops the cost per month sharply — the steepest decline anywhere in the range. The step from six to twelve adds considerably less.
