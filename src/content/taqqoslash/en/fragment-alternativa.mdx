Fragment is one of the official routes for buying Telegram Stars. It is not a card payment in UZS: it requires **TON cryptocurrency and a TON wallet**. StarsJoy and Uzgets let you pay for the same Stars in UZS with a local card.

## Which services are alternatives to Fragment?

Two local options are compared in Uzbekistan:

- **StarsJoy** — accepts UzCard, HUMO, Click and Payme;
- **Uzgets** — accepts UzCard, HUMO and Click.

Both only need the recipient's Telegram username. Never provide an account password, SMS code or 2FA code. If this is your first purchase, the [Stars buying guide](/en/qollanma/stars-qanday-sotib-olinadi) explains the sequence.

## How do Fragment and local services differ?

| Criterion | Fragment | StarsJoy / Uzgets |
|---|---|---|
| Payment currency | TON | UZS |
| What you need | TON wallet and TON | UzCard or HUMO |
| Fixed UZS price | not fixed in advance | shown before payment |
| Crypto operations | required | none |
| Recipient | Telegram account | Telegram username |
| Channel type | official Telegram platform | local reseller |

The main difference is the **payment route**, not the Stars. After delivery, Stars work identically inside Telegram for bots, mini apps, paid content and gifts.

## When does Fragment make sense?

Fragment makes sense when:

- you already have a TON wallet;
- TON is already available in your balance;
- you understand the terms of a crypto transaction;
- you want to buy directly through an official route.

If you must first acquire TON, move it into a wallet and then pay on Fragment solely to obtain Stars, one purchase becomes several separate operations.

## When is a local alternative more practical?

StarsJoy or Uzgets is more practical when:

- you have UzCard or HUMO but no international card or TON;
- you want to see the final price in UZS before paying;
- you do not want to set up a crypto wallet;
- you want to send Stars to someone else by username.

Compare current prices as well as convenience: the [Stars package comparison](/en/taqqoslash/stars-paketlari) explains why buying a larger package does not automatically produce a discount.
