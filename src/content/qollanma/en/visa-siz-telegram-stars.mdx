## No international card is required

For Stars purchases in Uzbekistan, choose a local payment method. The total is shown in UZS and can be paid with UzCard, HUMO, Click, or Payme.

## What you need

Only a Telegram username is required. A password, SMS code, or two-factor authentication code is never part of a normal purchase.
