Getting Telegram Premium in Uzbekistan does not require an international card. But the question here is not only whether Visa is needed — **how it is activated** also decides what happens to the subscription afterwards.

## Why no Visa is needed

A local service buys Premium from its own account and attaches it to yours **as a gift**. The payment relationship with Telegram is the service's, not yours. All that is asked of you is payment in UZS and the recipient's username.

On the official route the subscription binds to your App Store or Google Play account — and that account needs an international card, with the price calculated in foreign currency plus store commission.

## The consequence: it does not renew itself

This is the difference most often overlooked.

Premium activated as a gift **does not renew when the term ends**. Your card is not stored by the service and no surprise charge appears — but the subscription does not continue on its own either. A new term is bought manually.

A store subscription works the other way: it renews automatically, and unless cancelled, money is taken every period.

Which suits you better is a personal call. If control matters, no auto-renewal is an advantage. If you are worried about forgetting, put the expiry date in your calendar.

## If a subscription is already running

One of the services states this on its own page: the new term is **added** to the running subscription, so the remaining days are not burned.

This cannot be guaranteed for every service — confirm the condition with the one you choose before paying.
