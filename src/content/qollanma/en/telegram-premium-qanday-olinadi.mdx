## Choose a subscription term

Compare available 1, 3, 6 or 12-month plans. Look at the **cost per month**, not only the total. Current offers appear on the [Telegram Premium prices](/en/telegram-premium) page.

## Verify the username

Enter the username of the account that should receive Premium. A one-letter error may identify another user. The service does not need a Telegram password, SMS code or 2FA code.

## Payment: which card, which app

Recheck the term and the final amount, then pay.

**The card is a local UzCard or HUMO.** All three compared services work in UZS: there is no currency conversion and no international card is required.

**Click, Payme and Uzum are not separate payment systems** — they are the apps you send the transfer from. Services describe this differently: one states "via Click", another "a transfer from any app". In practice the card is still UzCard or HUMO, so **the app you choose does not change the final amount**.

Keep the receipt until activation is confirmed.

## What to do if the payment fails

Paying again is not the first step. The amount can be taken twice, and refunds take time.

Check the card first: sufficient funds, online payments enabled, and no confirmation waiting in your banking app. In most cases the problem sits with the card settings rather than the service.

If money left the account and nothing arrived, contact the service's official support with the receipt and order number. The procedure is set out on [paid but nothing arrived](/en/xavfsizlik/tolov-qilindi-mahsulot-kelmadi).

## Confirm activation

Check the Premium profile badge and Premium section in Telegram settings. If it does not activate within the stated window, do not pay again; contact official support with the receipt and order number.
