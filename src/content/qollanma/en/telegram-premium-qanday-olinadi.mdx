## Choose a subscription term

Compare available 1, 3, 6 or 12-month plans. Look at the **cost per month**, not only the total. Current offers appear on the [Telegram Premium prices](/en/telegram-premium) page.

## Verify the username

Enter the username of the account that should receive Premium. A one-letter error may identify another user. The service does not need a Telegram password, SMS code or 2FA code.

## Pay and keep the receipt

Recheck the term and final amount, then pay through UzCard, HUMO, Click or Payme. Keep the receipt until activation is confirmed.

## Confirm activation

Check the Premium profile badge and Premium section in Telegram settings. If it does not activate within the stated window, do not pay again; contact official support with the receipt and order number.
