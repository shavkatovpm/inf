Click is not the product seller in this guide. It is **the app used to make the UZS payment**. The chosen service delivers Premium or Stars. That distinction also tells you whom to contact if something goes wrong.

## Which services accept Click?

| Service | Premium | Stars | Published Click route |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| StarsJoy | yes | yes | Click accepted |
| Uzgets | yes | yes | Click accepted |
| PremiumSend | yes | no | Click transfer to the card issued by its bot |

Current amounts are kept on the [Telegram Premium comparison](/en/telegram-premium) and [Telegram Stars prices](/en/telegram-stars) pages. The service, product and package determine the price; the payment app's name does not.

## How do you pay through Click?

1. Open the Telegram bot from the service's official website.
2. Choose Premium or Stars, then select a subscription term or Stars amount.
3. Enter the exact username of the recipient account.
4. Open the Click payment or card details issued by the bot.
5. Check the **recipient, amount and payment note**, then confirm.
6. Save the receipt and verify that the product appears in Telegram.

Open the profile and compare every character before entering a username. The service does not need a Telegram password, SMS code or 2FA code. Compare suspicious requests against the [fraud warning signs](/en/xavfsizlik/firibgarlik-belgilari).

## When does Click charge a fee?

Click's official tariffs normally list product and service payments through the system at **0%**, while a card-to-card transfer is a separately priced operation. A bot may launch a billing payment or provide card details, so a blanket promise of “no fee” would be inaccurate.

The reliable figure is the **final debit total** on Click's confirmation screen. If it differs from the bot's order total, do not confirm until the service explains the difference.

## What if payment does not go through?

Do not repeat the same payment immediately. First:

- check whether Click history says successful, pending or declined;
- check the card balance and that SMS notifications are connected;
- make sure the bot invoice has not expired;
- if money was debited, retain the receipt and order number.

There is a separate [diagnostic checklist](/en/xavfsizlik/tolov-qilindi-mahsulot-kelmadi) for money debited without delivery. If Click says successful but the order is unfulfilled, contact the seller first; if Click itself shows an operation error, contact Click support.
