The process of buying Telegram Stars is almost identical across local services. Below is the full sequence and what to watch for at each step.

## Step 1. Open t.me/starsjoybot

Open the service's official bot and press `/start`. The bot's username must match the one published on the service's site — do not trust similarly named bots from search, as imitations exist.

## Step 2. Select the Stars section

The menu usually offers Stars, Premium and Gifts. Choose Stars.

## Step 3. Set the amount

Ready-made packages (50, 100, 500, 1000…) or a custom amount are offered. To know the price in advance, use the [calculator](/en/kalkulyator).

## Step 4. Enter the username

This is the most important step. You enter the **username of the person** whose balance should receive the Stars.

> At this stage a trustworthy service **does not ask for a password, SMS code or 2FA password**. If it does, stop the purchase. More: [fraud warning signs](/en/xavfsizlik/firibgarlik-belgilari).

Check the username carefully — if it is wrong, the Stars go to someone else and are hard to recover.

## Step 5. Payment

Payment is made in UZS: UzCard, HUMO, Click or Payme. Keep the receipt — you will need it if anything goes wrong.

## Step 6. Verify

Open Telegram settings and check the Stars balance. Delivery usually takes a few minutes.

## What to check

| Stage | Check |
|---|---|
| Bot | Does the username match the one on the site |
| Username | Entered without typos |
| Password request | There should be none |
| Payment | Official method, receipt saved |
| Delivery | Visible on the balance |
