## Verify the transaction

Open the operation in your bank or payment app. “Successful”, “pending” and “cancelled” are different states. Record the amount, time and transaction identifier.

## Check the order details

Verify the username, product and package. A one-character username error may send the product to another account. Do not place a second order before the stated delivery window ends, as this can cause a duplicate charge.

## What to send support

The order number, payment time, amount, recipient username and receipt are sufficient. Do not send a full card number, CVV, SMS code, Telegram password or 2FA code.

## If support does not respond

Once the service’s response window has passed, contact the payment provider or card-issuing bank and obtain a case number. Refund options depend on the payment method and service terms; a bank case does not guarantee an automatic refund.
