The Telegram Stars and Premium market has both trustworthy services and fraudsters. Telling them apart is not hard — the signs below hold almost every time.

## 1. Requesting a password, SMS code or 2FA

This is the single most important sign. **Delivering Stars or Premium never requires account access.** Delivery happens by username alone.

If a service offers to "sign in for you" and asks for a password or SMS code, the goal is not delivery — it is taking over your account. Handing over an SMS code loses you the account, which is then used to defraud your contacts.

## 2. A price far below the market

If a price sits 30–40% below the market average, question it. Reseller margins are limited — an unusually low price usually means one of two things: a prepayment-and-disappear scheme, or goods bought with stolen cards (later reversed by Telegram).

## 3. No public reviews

Screenshots are not proof — they are trivial to fake. The real signal is a **public reviews page with visible dates**, carrying both positive and negative entries.

## 4. Payment only to a personal card

A legitimate service offers Click, Payme or a transfer to an official account. If "send it to this card" is the only option, the scheme is unprotected — recovering such a payment is nearly impossible.

## 5. Manufactured urgency

"Offer ends in 10 minutes", "only 3 slots left" — artificial pressure exists to stop you thinking it through. A real service publishes stable, visible pricing.

## 6. A new source with no history

Be careful if the domain or channel is only days old. Time in market is not a perfect signal, but it is a useful one.

## 7. No support channel

There must be somewhere to turn when something goes wrong. A single personal account that can block you at any moment is a risk.

## Quick checklist

| Signal | Safe | Risky |
|---|---|---|
| Password / SMS request | never asked | asked |
| Price | close to market | far below |
| Reviews | public, dated | absent or screenshots |
| Payment | Click/Payme/official | personal card |
| Support | dedicated channel | none |

If two or more of these line up, it is better to stop the purchase.
